Frequently Asked Questions
What is Cashibra? +
Cashibra is a gamified learning project where teens learn money, skills, and financial basics through games, quests, and boss battles.
Is this website finished? +
No. This is an early demo. Many features like missions, coins, streaks, and learning levels will be added later.
Is Cashibra safe? +
Yes. Cashibra does not collect sensitive personal data. It is purely an educational experiment.
Will I earn real money? +
No. The goal is to teach financial thinking and skills through interactive learning.
Who made Cashibra? +
Cashibra was created by Aarav Mahule, a young learner experimenting with teaching finance through games.
